STAFFING SERVICES AGREEMENT — Ohio Community Care Authority (State of Ohio)
Contractor: Harbor Point Staffing Group
 
1. PERIOD OF PERFORMANCE
Base period begins March 1, 2027 and ends February 29, 2028.
One (1) option year available. The Authority must provide written notice of intent
to exercise the option year no later than December 1, 2027 (Section 1.2).
 
2. DELIVERABLES
Contractor shall submit Monthly Status Reports by the 7th business day of each month.
Quarterly staffing compliance reports due within 20 days of quarter end.
 
3. PERSONNEL
Contractor shall provide licensed nursing staff (LPN and RN) at client facilities in
Ohio. All personnel classifications must conform to Ohio state licensing law.
 
4. NON-DISCLOSURE
The NDA executed as Exhibit B remains in force until June 30, 2029.
 
5. TERMINATION
Either party may terminate with 90 days written notice.
